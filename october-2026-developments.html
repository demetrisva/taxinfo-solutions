Cyprus Tax Developments: September 15–October 4, 2026

What changed in the last three weeks, who it affects and what to do before the October and November deadlines.

Back to Briefing Hub
Monthly update 8 sections Official government sources

Between September 15 and October 4, 2026 the Tax Department published four announcements, the Cabinet approved the 2027 State Budget and a seven-point package on prices, and a new VAT order appeared in the Official Gazette. Three of these change dates or rates that businesses use this quarter: the annual PAYE return, the zero-rate VAT lists and the way refund bank details are registered.

In force now vs. approved only: the PAYE extension, the IBAN procedure and the zero-rate order K.D.P. 354/2026 are in force. The heating-oil excise cut, 9% VAT on household photovoltaics and the 2027 extension of the basic-goods zero rate are Cabinet decisions that still need their own legal instruments. The 2027 Budget is before Parliament.

1. Annual PAYE return for 2025 extended to November 30

Employers · In force · Announced September 16

September 16, 2026

By decision of the Tax Commissioner, the deadline for employers' annual income tax withholding return (PAYE, form TD7) for tax year 2025 moves from September 30 to November 30, 2026. This is the second extension this year: the date had already moved from May 31 to September 30.

A return filed after November 30 is late and, under Article 50A(a) of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law 4/1978, attracts a fixed charge:

EmployerCharge for late annual PAYE return
Individual (natural person)EUR 150
Legal person with turnover or assets above EUR 1,000,000EUR 500
Any other legal personEUR 250

Worked example. A small company with EUR 400,000 turnover and EUR 300,000 of assets that submits on December 2 pays EUR 250. Its parent, with EUR 3 million of assets, would pay EUR 500 for the same delay. These are per-return charges; any underpaid withholding is dealt with separately.

  • Reconcile total 2025 payroll, withholding paid and employee certificates (TD63) before submitting.
  • Fix any month where withholding paid does not match the monthly return.
  • Do not wait for a third extension; none has been announced.

Tax Department announcement, September 16, 2026 (Greek)

2. Zero-rate VAT renewed and extended to bread, milk, coffee, sugar and baby food

Retail and food businesses · In force · Order K.D.P. 354/2026

October 2, 2026

Order K.D.P. 354/2026, published in the Official Gazette on September 30, 2026 (Gazette No. 6050), amends the VAT Law. The Tax Department explained its scope on October 2:

Products0% from0% until
Fresh, chilled or frozen beef, pork, lamb, goat, poultry, rabbit and edible offal (CN 0201–0204, ex 0206, 0207, ex 0208)October 1, 2026May 31, 2027
Fresh, chilled or frozen fish, cuttlefish, squid and octopus (CN 0302–0304, ex 0307)October 1, 2026May 31, 2027
Bread of all kinds, fresh or frozenOctober 12, 2026May 31, 2027
Milk, including condensed, long-life, flavoured and plant-basedOctober 12, 2026May 31, 2027
Coffee in any form (not ready-to-drink beverages)October 12, 2026May 31, 2027
SugarOctober 12, 2026May 31, 2027
Baby food (not snacks)October 12, 2026May 31, 2027

What stays taxed. Meat and fish preparations remain at 5%: marinated, smoked or wine-cured meat, sheftalia, burgers, nuggets, sausages, lountza, gyros, canned or breaded fish. Pastries, dried bread and bread with added ingredients such as raisins, nuts or herbs are excluded, as are coffee drinks served hot or cold and children's snacks such as crisps, sweets, chocolate, ice cream and yoghurt pots.

Worked example. A bakery sells a white loaf for EUR 2.10 including 5% VAT, so EUR 0.10 is VAT. From October 12 the same loaf is zero-rated. If the bakery keeps the price at EUR 2.10, its net takings rise to EUR 2.10; if it passes the relief on, the price becomes EUR 2.00. A raisin loaf on the same shelf stays at 5%. Zero-rated sales are still reported on the VAT return as taxable supplies, and input VAT on costs remains recoverable.

  • Change till and ERP tax codes for the new products before trading opens on Monday, October 12.
  • Keep the meat and fish codes at 0% without a break from October 1.
  • Keep the October 2 notice and the order with your VAT file for the period.

Tax Department notice, October 2, 2026 (Greek) · VAT operations briefing

3. Cabinet package of September 30: what is approved but not yet law

Households and businesses · Approved · Instruments pending

September 30, 2026

The Council of Ministers approved seven proposals from the Minister of Finance with a stated cost of EUR 70 million, or about EUR 160 million including measures already running. The tax-related ones are:

  • Basic-goods zero rate for all of 2027: fresh fruit, vegetables, infant milk, children's and adult nappies and feminine hygiene products. The current order runs to December 31, 2026.
  • Heating-oil excise: reduced from 7.4 to 2.1 cents per litre from November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027.
  • Household photovoltaic systems: VAT reduced from 19% to 9%, effective from publication of an order in the Official Gazette.
  • Meat, fish and the new staples: the zero rate now in force under K.D.P. 354/2026 (section 2).

The package also renews the electricity subsidy for vulnerable and commercial consumers for 2027, pays a one-off EUR 200 to 53,511 vulnerable beneficiaries and raises the mountain-area allowance.

Worked example: heating oil. A household buying 1,000 litres in November would pay EUR 21 of excise instead of EUR 74, a reduction of EUR 53 before VAT. Photovoltaics: on a EUR 6,000 (net) rooftop system, VAT falls from EUR 1,140 to EUR 540 once the order is published. The rate generally depends on when the supply takes place, so ask the installer which rate will appear on the invoice before paying a deposit.

Council of Ministers and Ministry of Finance announcement, September 30, 2026 (Greek)

4. Refund bank details only through Tax For All from October 5

All taxpayers expecting refunds · In force from October 5

September 22, 2026

From October 5, 2026, requests to register or change the bank account (IBAN) used for tax refunds must be made through the Tax For All portal after logging in with CY Login. The taxpayer submits Form T.F. 2008 electronically together with an IBAN confirmation issued by the bank. The account must be in the beneficiary's own name. Requests sent any other way, including by email, will be rejected.

An entity that objectively cannot obtain CY Login, because it cannot be identified in that system, may submit a request in TFA without logging in, using a signed Form T.F. 2008-S. The exception does not cover entities that simply have not set up CY Login yet.

  • Before filing a 2025 return that will produce a refund, check the IBAN held by the Department.
  • Ask the bank for an IBAN confirmation letter in the account holder's name.
  • Companies without CY Login should start registration now rather than rely on the exception.

Tax Department announcement, September 22, 2026 (Greek)

5. 2027 State Budget submitted to Parliament

Fiscal outlook · Before Parliament

September 24, 2026

The Cabinet approved the 2027 State Budget and the 2027–2029 Medium-Term Fiscal Framework on September 16, and the Minister of Finance submitted them to the House of Representatives on September 24. The Ministry projects a fiscal surplus of about 4% of GDP in 2027, public debt falling to 45.2% of GDP by the end of 2027, growth of 2.9%, unemployment of about 3.8% and inflation of 2.5%.

The Budget statement does not announce new income tax rates. The EUR 22,000 tax-free threshold and the 2026 reform deductions continue to apply. Planning for 2027 should still start from the 2026 bands and deductions unless Parliament adopts something different.

Ministry of Finance statement, September 24, 2026

6. FATCA/CRS guide for administrative service providers withdrawn

ASPs, trustees and fiduciaries · In force · September 17

September 17, 2026

The Tax Department withdrew its June 2017 guide Cyprus Administrative Service Providers (ASPs): Are They FIs under FATCA and CRS with immediate effect. Entities should no longer rely on it to decide whether they are financial institutions. Instead, they should apply paragraph 10ter of the OECD Consolidated Text of the CRS (2025): for the gross income test, all remuneration for an entity's relevant activities counts, even if it is paid to another entity. For example, when a law or accounting firm sets up a trust and appoints a corporate trustee, the fees the client pays the firm for trustee services count toward the trustee's own test.

References to the same point in the May 2020 Guidance Notes no longer apply; the Department says the Guidance Notes will be updated for the amended CRS. It also reminds reporting entities that it can audit CRS/DAC2 compliance and impose administrative penalties.

  • Re-run FI classification for corporate trustees, nominee and custodian entities in ASP groups.
  • Document how group fees are allocated to each entity's gross income test.
  • Register and report where reclassification makes an entity a reporting FI.

Tax Department announcement, September 17, 2026 (Greek)

7. Still running: fuel excise relief and the 2025 individual return

Reminder

October 4, 2026

The motor-fuel excise reduction of 8.33 cents per litre continues to November 30, 2026 under the September 10 Cabinet decision covered in the September update. The 2025 individual income tax return is due October 31, 2026 through TAXISnet. October 31 is a Saturday, so plan to file during the week before. On September 28 the Tax Department published an explanatory video on completing the 2025 return.
