Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

How we use cookies

Ad serving is currently paused. The table describes advertising technologies that may be used if ads are enabled after certified CMP verification, and infrastructure technologies that may be used by the hosting provider. It is not a claim that every listed cookie is set on every visit.

Cookie Name Provider Purpose
NID / AEC / IDE / related ad cookies and local storage Google AdSense Ad delivery, personalisation where permitted, frequency control, measurement, fraud prevention, and revenue reporting where ads are shown.
Consent message storage Google AdSense / certified CMP Stores advertising consent choices for visitors in regions where a consent message is required.
__cf_bm Cloudflare Security. Used to identify trusted web traffic and prevent bot attacks.

Local storage and offline pages

The site stores installation-prompt dismissal timestamps (14-day install snooze; 30-day iOS hint snooze) and caches pages and assets for offline use. It does not save calculator inputs. Expired timestamps may remain until cleared. Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove local storage and cached pages.

Advertising consent choices

No advertising consent banner is currently active in this release because ad loading is paused. Before advertising resumes, a certified CMP must provide the applicable choices and a working way to revisit or withdraw them.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, advertising consent must be collected through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised AdSense ads are served. The consent message for this domain should let you accept, reject, or manage advertising choices where required by law.

You can learn more about ad cookies and controls here: Google Ads technologies.
