Cyprus Tax Updates: November 2026

Confirmed VAT changes, conditional filing relief and the next compliance actions.

Back to Briefing Hub

1. Individual returns: conditional November relief

The October 31 statutory date has passed. For the 2025 individual return without accounts, the October 19 Tax Department notice grants relief from interest and financial charges if both the return and the corresponding taxes and contributions are completed by November 30, 2026. This is conditional relief, not a replacement statutory deadline. The notice warns that changing a final TAXISnet submission back to provisional status may be unavailable from November 1.

Action: retain the submission receipt and payment proof together. A filing alone does not satisfy the payment condition. Ask the Department how to correct an already-final return if required.

2. Basic-goods VAT extension: order confirmed

The October 19 Tax Department notice confirms K.D.P. 362/2026, dated October 16: the defined infant-milk, nappy, feminine-hygiene and listed fruit-and-vegetable schedule is extended for January 1–December 31, 2027. Keep it separate from meat, fish and the October staple additions, whose schedule ends May 31, 2027.

Action: link each SKU to the relevant list and decree. Do not extend every food product to December 2027 or schedule an automatic January rate reset for goods covered by the extension.

3. Pillar Two: first-cycle November 30 notice

The October 27 Tax Department announcement sets November 30, 2026 for the listed first-cycle returns, notifications, communications and top-up tax payment for Cyprus constituent entities or joint ventures whose first financial year begins between December 31, 2023 and December 30, 2024. It also provides relief for covered obligations otherwise due earlier, if completed by that date.

Action: confirm the entity, first financial year, required forms and payment with the group tax team. This is a scoped Pillar Two notice, not a general extension of every company tax obligation.

4. TFA maintenance: elapsed window, no general extension

The October 29 announcement scheduled unavailability from November 2 at 21:00 to November 3 at 12:00, Cyprus local time. That announced window has passed; it does not establish current service health or extend deadlines.

Check access, retain acknowledgements and escalate failed submissions promptly.

5. Controlled transactions and innovative SME investment

The circular register lists Circular 2026/05, dated October 12, revising return percentages in paragraphs 14, 18 and 25 of Circular 6/2023. Review affected financing files against the circular before reusing prior assumptions.

The State Aid Commissioner’s announcement makes the proposed June 30, 2027 innovative-SME investment deduction extension conditional on parliamentary passage and publication. Approval of the state-aid amendment alone does not enact the tax deduction.

Review scope and unresolved topics

This targeted review checked the Tax Department announcement feed and linked October notices, the circular register, current reform material and relevant filing events as available on November 6, 2026. The linked Greek official notices define the scope and conditions; confirm the original text for a taxpayer-specific decision.

The 2027 State Budget and proposed innovative-SME changes must be distinguished from enacted law. No current implementing instrument was verified in this review for household-photovoltaic VAT, heating-oil excise or the October Pillar Two amending bill; check the Gazette before relying on an announced change.
