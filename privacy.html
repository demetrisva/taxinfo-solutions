Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026
Applies to: taxinfo.solutions and all pages published under that domain.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use TaxInfo Solutions, how it is used, and what choices you have. We have written this in plain language because we believe you should understand it without needing a lawyer.

1. Who We Are

Site operator and data controller: Demetris Valakos, operating TaxInfo Solutions. Contact: info@taxinfo.solutions. The articles and calculators are available free of charge. This is an independent website, not a government service.

TaxInfo Solutions is an independent editorial publication focused on Cyprus tax information. We are not a data company, a financial services firm, or a software platform. We publish articles, briefings, and calculator tools to help individuals, payroll teams, and business operators understand Cyprus tax rules for 2026.

Editorial contact: info@taxinfo.solutions

2. Calculator Inputs: No Data Collected

The salary calculator and business profit estimator on this site operate entirely on your device using client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • The salary, income, or profit figures you type are processed locally in your browser.
  • No financial inputs are sent to our servers, stored in a database, or accessible to us.
  • The site does not save these inputs. Your browser may restore form values through its own history or session features.

We designed the calculators this way deliberately. A tax information site should not hold your financial details, and ours does not.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Like all websites, TaxInfo Solutions collects some technical information automatically when you visit. This is standard infrastructure behaviour, not active data collection by our editorial team.

Hosting and Infrastructure Logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served via Cloudflare's content delivery network. These services may log standard request data including your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, and pages visited with timestamps. This data is retained by the infrastructure provider for security and performance purposes and is subject to their own privacy policies. We do not have routine access to individual log entries and do not use this data to identify you personally.

Service Worker and Caching

This site uses a service worker for performance caching (PWA functionality). The service worker caches static assets such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files locally on your device so that pages load faster. No personal data is stored by the service worker.

4. Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies for tracking, analytics, or personalisation. However, the following third-party services used on this site may set cookies:

Cloudflare Infrastructure Cookies

Cloudflare may set the cookie __cf_bm to identify and manage bot traffic. This is a security cookie that expires within 30 minutes of inactivity. It does not track you personally across the web.

Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

Ad requests are currently paused while the published Google consent message is validated and site approval is pending. Selected editorial pages load the Google AdSense tag to deliver the consent message. Google receives standard network request data; existing Google cookies may be read when its scripts load. The publisher verification meta tag and ads.txt do not themselves serve advertisements. If advertising is enabled in a future update, selected editorial pages may display Google AdSense ads. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies, use local storage, read device identifiers, process IP addresses, and use similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, limit frequency, and support ad personalisation where permitted.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, AdSense advertising should be served only with a Google-certified consent management platform configured for this domain. That consent message is expected to explain the ad technology providers involved and allow you to accept, reject, or manage advertising choices where required by law.

Advertising does not influence our editorial topics, source selection, conclusions, calculators, or page rankings. We do not accept sponsored editorial placements or paid rankings.

You can review and control Google's use of your data through the following resources:

  • How Google uses cookies in advertising
  • Google Ad Settings — opt out or customise your preferences
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out

For more information about how we use cookies specifically, see our Cookie Policy.

Browser storage and external libraries

Installation-prompt dismissal timestamps are stored locally for 14 days (install prompt) or 30 days (iOS hint). Expired entries may remain until site data is cleared. Cache Storage holds previously visited pages and assets until refreshed, evicted or cleared. Clear site data in your browser to remove these items.

When a salary chart is requested, the browser loads a version-pinned Chart.js library from jsDelivr, which receives normal network request details. Financial inputs are not sent with that request.

5. Google Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font file, Google's servers receive your IP address and browser information as part of the standard HTTP request. Google may log this for diagnostic and security purposes. We do not control this data exchange. If you prefer to prevent Google Fonts requests, you can block them using browser privacy extensions.

6. Analytics and Advertising Measurement

TaxInfo Solutions does not currently use Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, or any other standalone visitor analytics platform. We do not run our own analytics profile of individual page views, session duration, or user journeys.

Google AdSense may still perform ad measurement, fraud prevention, frequency capping, reporting, and revenue accounting on pages where ads are enabled. Those activities are controlled by Google and its advertising technology partners, subject to the consent choices and legal settings applicable to your location.

7. External Links

Our briefings and source panels contain links to official government websites, Ministry of Finance publications, Tax For All notices, and other external resources. When you click an external link, you leave taxinfo.solutions and the privacy practices of the destination site apply. We are not responsible for the data practices of linked sites, including official government portals.

8. Children

This site is intended for adults engaged in professional, business, or personal tax planning. It is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Data Retention

If you email us, your message and contact details are processed to respond to the enquiry and retain any necessary correction record. We do not solicit private tax files. Infrastructure log data held by Cloudflare and GitHub Pages is subject to those providers' respective retention policies.

10. Your Rights

If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, if you have contacted us by email), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that data by contacting info@taxinfo.solutions. We normally respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions. Email providers process correspondence to deliver and store messages; we retain it only as necessary for the enquiry, relevant records and legal obligations.

You may also request restriction or object to processing where applicable, request portability where the conditions apply, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You can complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Hosting and communications providers may process data outside the EEA. Their applicable privacy notices describe locations and transfer safeguards. See GitHub privacy, Cloudflare privacy and Google partner-site data use.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the services we use. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We recommend checking this page periodically if you use the site regularly.
