Cyprus Developments: August 18–September 14, 2026

Five official announcements, what each one changes and what it does not.

Back to Briefing Hub
Monthly update 6 sections Official government sources

This update covers official announcements between the August 17 refresh and September 14, 2026. It separates decisions already in force from proposals that still need Parliament. For later changes, including the PAYE extension to November 30 and the new zero-rate VAT order, see the October update.

1. Motor-fuel excise reduction extended to November 30

Households and transport · In force

September 10, 2026

The Ministry of Finance announced Cabinet approval to continue the motor-fuel excise reduction through November 30, 2026, replacing the September 17 expiry. The relief is 8.33 cents per litre for petrol and road diesel.

Worked example. A delivery business using 3,000 litres of diesel a month pays EUR 249.90 less excise per month while the reduction applies, or roughly EUR 600 over the 74-day extension from September 17 to November 30, before VAT. A tax reduction is not a guarantee of a particular pump price.

Keep the excise measure separate from VAT relief on food. Separately, the September 30 Cabinet package plans a heating-oil excise cut from November 1; see the October update.

Ministry of Finance statement, September 10, 2026 (Greek)

2. Child-benefit changes approved as a bill

Families · Proposal before Parliament

September 10, 2026

The Deputy Ministry of Social Welfare announced Cabinet approval of an amending child-benefit bill intended to apply from January 1, 2027. It proposes wider income criteria and higher support; the Deputy Ministry estimates that 17,000 more families and 23,500 more children would become eligible. The bill must be passed by Parliament before it applies.

Child benefit is a social-welfare payment. It is separate from the 2026 income-tax deduction for dependent children (EUR 1,000 for the first child, EUR 1,250 for the second and EUR 1,500 for each further child, subject to family-income conditions). Do not change a 2026 TD59 because of the benefit proposal.

Deputy Ministry of Social Welfare announcement (Greek) · Family deduction checklist

3. Tax-system interruption on September 11 and 12

Filers · Past event

September 9, 2026

The Tax Department announced a temporary interruption of specified computer systems on Friday September 11 and Saturday September 12, 2026. The notice did not extend any deadline.

If a submission or payment was attempted during those days, check that it has an acknowledgement. If it failed, keep the error message and timestamp and contact the Department; do not assume a deadline moved because access was interrupted.

Tax Department notice, September 9, 2026 (Greek)

4. Honduras bond yield published for notional interest deduction

Companies claiming NID · In force

August 24, 2026

The Tax Department published the 10-year government-bond yield of Honduras in US dollars at December 31, 2025. Under Article 9B of the Income Tax Law, the NID reference rate for new equity invested in a country is that country's 10-year bond yield plus five percentage points, so the figure matters to Cyprus companies whose new equity is deployed in Honduras.

Worked example. If the published yield were 9.0%, the reference rate would be 14.0%. On EUR 1 million of qualifying new equity, notional interest before the statutory cap would be EUR 140,000. Use the actual published figure, and remember that NID is also limited to 80% of taxable profit from the new equity.

Tax Department notice, August 24, 2026 (Greek) · NID and IP Box briefing

5. Tax residence certificates for imaging centres

Healthcare operators · In force

August 20, 2026

The Tax Department published a procedure for the tax residence certificate required by Article 9(2)(d) of the Imaging Centres Law, N.35(I)/2026. Applicants email their registered district tax office with the appropriate declaration, supporting documents and confirmation of their administrative and/or scientific responsibility at the centre. The certificate is issued only for submission to the Ministry of Health.

This sector certificate does not change the general 183-day or 60-day residency tests and does not replace a certificate for double-tax-treaty purposes.

Tax Department procedure and forms (Greek)

6. What happened next

Follow-up

October 19, 2026

Two dates that were current when this update was published have since changed. The 2025 annual PAYE return, then due September 30, was extended on September 16 to November 30, 2026. The zero rate on fresh meat and fish, then due to end on September 30, continues to May 31, 2027 and now also covers bread, milk, coffee, sugar and baby food from October 12. Both are explained in the October update and the tax calendar.
